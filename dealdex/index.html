iOS crypto tracker

Watch DEX listings the moment they land.

DealDex is a non-custodial iOS tracker for DEX listings. New pair, new pool, fresh liquidity -- you see it before the chatter catches up. When you want to act, the app routes you to the best venue across chains and hands the swap to your wallet. The app never holds a key.

What's inside

  • Multi-chain feed. Ethereum, Solana, BNB, Base, and a handful of long-tail chains. New pairs and fresh liquidity show up within seconds.
  • Listing alerts. Per-token alerts fire from your device. No email, no account, no relay.
  • Venue routing. Tap a token, the app shows the live spread across supported DEXes and routes the swap suggestion through the best venue.
  • Liquidity scoring. Pair depth, holder concentration, and lock status surfaced at a glance. The app does not score quality; it surfaces the inputs.
  • Watchlist. Pin tokens; the feed highlights anything that touches them.

How it works

DealDex reads public onchain data and DEX aggregator quotes. The app never asks for your seed phrase or your wallet key. When you choose to swap, the app hands the route to your installed wallet -- you confirm there, and your wallet signs and broadcasts. Custody stays with you, end to end.

  1. Browse the feed; tap a pair to see venue routing.
  2. Choose a venue. The app hands the swap route to your wallet.
  3. Confirm in your wallet. The app stays out of the signing path.

What it doesn't do

  • Centralised exchanges. CEX listings are out of scope for v1. The app tracks DEX pairs.
  • Custody. No embedded wallet, no key storage, no in-app signing. The app is read-only until you hand off to your wallet.
  • Token recommendations. The app surfaces liquidity, depth, and spread; the buy-or-skip call is yours.

Privacy

DealDex reads public onchain data and DEX aggregator quotes. The app never asks for a seed phrase or private key. Crash reports are opt-in and scrubbed of identifiers. Full notice at /privacy.

Need help?

The most common hiccup is the wallet handoff not opening the right app. The support page covers the four checks that catch 90% of swap-routing issues; if those don't help, write to feedback@simplewithus.com with the chain, the pair, and your wallet app.
