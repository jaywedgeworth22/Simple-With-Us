ContactLogo

Support

Permissions, roster matching, and known-issue reference. If the four checks below don't fix it, send the output to feedback@simplewithus.com and we'll come back the same day.

The four checks that catch 90% of match misses

  1. iOS: Contacts permission. Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Contacts > ContactLogo. If it's set to "None" or "Limited", the app can't read names. Grant "Full Access".
  2. Roster: company name match. The roster column header must be company. Free-text variations like "Inc." or "Corp" don't get stripped; if the contact says "Acme, Inc." and your roster says "Acme", they won't match. Normalize in the roster first.
  3. Contacts: write source. iOS writes back to one source at a time. Settings > Contacts > Default Account -- pick the source you want logos on. Other sources are skipped, not overwritten.
  4. Storage: undo log. If a previous batch went sideways, open Undo Log and roll back to before that run. Logs are capped at the last 20 batches; older runs are pruned.

Crash log or "Application keeps closing"

If ContactLogo crashes immediately on launch or while writing a batch, pull a Console log from the Mac (Console.app > iPhone > ContactLogo) and send the last ~200 lines to feedback@simplewithus.com. If you can also repro the crash, note the steps and the roster file you were using.

Brand runs but no contacts change

This is almost always a roster-format issue. Confirm the header row is company,domain and that there's at least one row. If you're using the curated roster, open the in-app preview and confirm the company you expect is listed.

Sending logs to support

The app's Settings screen has a "Send Logs" action (under Diagnostics). It bundles the last few minutes into a single zip, scrubbed of your address book, and opens a prefilled email to feedback@simplewithus.com. Attach the zip; we respond usually within a business day.

Contact

feedback@simplewithus.com -- open a thread, don't worry about templates. Link to your TestFlight build if you have it (Settings > Version > Build).
