Native iOS contact branding

Brand every contact with their company logo.

ContactLogo puts a real company logo on every contact in your iPhone address book. One-time batch, fully on-device, with an undo log for safety.

Visit contactlogo.com Support Privacy

What's inside

  • Bulk brand. Match every contact against a curated company roster in one pass; missing logos are flagged, not guessed.
  • On-device. Your address book never leaves your phone. No upload step, no account, no cloud round-trip.
  • Undo log. Every batch is recorded with a timestamp; one tap restores the previous state if a contact looks wrong.
  • Source-aware. Respects iCloud, Google, and local sources; only writes to the source you choose.

How it works

ContactLogo reads your address book with permission, matches companies against a curated roster, and writes the logo back as the contact's image. The match is deterministic, so re-running on the same roster is idempotent.

  1. Grant Contacts permission on first run. ContactLogo only reads; nothing uploads.
  2. Pick a roster -- the default curated set, or drop in your own CSV.
  3. Tap Brand. The app writes matches and shows a summary. Open the Undo log to roll back.

Privacy: ContactLogo is fully on-device. The address book and the matches stay on your phone. Full notice at /privacy.

What it doesn't do

  • Auto-detect companies. We don't infer company from email domain; the match comes from the roster you choose.
  • Background sync. No daemon, no push, no scheduled refresh. Run a batch when you want one.
  • Social avatars. We don't pull profile photos from third-party networks.

Privacy

ContactLogo does not transmit anything to Simple With Us. The app reads your contacts on-device, writes logos, and that's it. Crash reports are opt-in and scrubbed of identifiers. Full notice at /privacy.

Need help?

A match can occasionally skip a contact if the company name in your roster differs from the one stored on the contact. The support page covers the four checks that catch 90% of match misses; if those don't help, write to feedback@simplewithus.com with a sample contact (no real data needed) and which roster you used.
