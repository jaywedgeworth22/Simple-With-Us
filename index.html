What you should know

The same handful of values across the whole family.

No data locked in. Every app exports its core data as CSV or JSON. If we shut down tomorrow, your work leaves with you in a single tap.

No telemetry by default. Only what each app explicitly needs to function. Crash reports are opt-in and never tied to your identity unless you ask.

On-device by default. Where a feature needs network access, it tells you; you can deny it. There is no analytics or ad SDK on this site.

One support address. Every app's "App Review" contact rolls up to feedback@simplewithus.com. You'll get a real human, usually within a day.
