Autorotate.Codes

Support

Per-app rules, Bluetooth triggers, and known-issue reference. If the four checks below don't fix it, send the output to feedback@simplewithus.com and we'll come back the same day.

The four checks that catch 90% of "rule did nothing" reports

  1. Rule toggle is on. Rules screen shows the toggle on the right. If it's off, the rule is defined but never fires. Toggle on.
  2. App in foreground when you test. The rule fires on app-foreground events. If the app is already open, leave it and reopen. Foreground events drive the rule, not background motion.
  3. Bluetooth device actually connected. For device-triggered rules, confirm the device shows as Connected in iOS Settings > Bluetooth, not just Paired. Many car mounts stay Paired but never connect until the engine starts.
  4. App overrides orientation. Some apps (camera, most video players) take over orientation lock. The rule fires (Diagnostics shows it); the app ignores it. That's the app, not us.

Crash log or "Application keeps closing"

If Autorotate.Codes crashes immediately on launch or after a specific screen, pull a Console log from the Mac (Console.app > iPhone > Autorotate.Codes) and send the last ~200 lines to feedback@simplewithus.com. If you can repro, note the exact steps and the iOS version (Settings > General > About).

Rule fired but the screen did not rotate

The Diagnostics screen shows recent firings. If a rule fired and the screen did not change, the targeted app almost certainly locks orientation itself. That's an app-level constraint we cannot override without using private APIs. Email the app's developer; the most you can do is open the app, leave it, and reopen.

Sending logs to support

The app's Settings screen has a "Send Logs" action (under Diagnostics). It bundles the last few minutes into a single zip, scrubbed of your rule names, and opens a prefilled email to feedback@simplewithus.com. Attach the zip; we respond usually within a business day.

Contact

feedback@simplewithus.com -- open a thread, don't worry about templates. Note your iOS version and the app you were testing the rule against.
