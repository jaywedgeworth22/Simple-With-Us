Support

One inbox for the whole family.

Every Simple With Us app's App Review contact rolls up to the same address. If something's broken, missing, or just confusing, send it through here.

feedback@simplewithus.com · replies usually within a business day, often sooner.

What helps us help you

  • The app name and version you're on (Settings > About, or the TestFlight build if you have it).
  • Your iOS or macOS version.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened.
  • A screenshot if the bug is visual.

If you prefer per-app support

Each app's TestFlight page lists the developer's name (Simple With Us); open that page for the per-app support link.
