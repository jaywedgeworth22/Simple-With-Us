Native macOS contact branding

Brand contacts on your Mac, too.

Mac sibling of ContactLogo. Drag-drop your company roster to brand contacts, with a single undo log shared across the Address Book source.

Visit contactlogo.com/mac Support Privacy

What's inside

  • Drag-drop roster. Drop a CSV or a folder of images straight onto the window. The app infers matches and previews before writing.
  • Shared undo log. One undo log per Address Book source -- visible from iOS if you also run ContactLogo there.
  • Image fallback. If a company isn't in the curated roster, drop a PNG/SVG into the Roster folder and ContactLogo uses it for matching by file name.
  • macOS-native. Uses Contacts.framework, sandboxed, no helper process. Runs as a regular app, not a menu-bar daemon.

How it works

ContactLogo for Mac reads your Address Book with permission, matches companies against the roster you provide, and writes the chosen image back as the contact's photo. The match is deterministic, so re-running is safe.

  1. Grant Contacts permission on first launch. System Settings > Privacy & Security > Contacts.
  2. Drop your roster into the window -- CSV or a folder of named images.
  3. Review the preview, then click Brand. The undo log captures the pre-state for one-tap rollback.

Privacy: ContactLogo for Mac is fully on-device. The Address Book and the matches stay on your Mac. Full notice at /privacy.

What it doesn't do

  • Cross-source merges. We write to one source at a time. Duplicates across sources are flagged, not collapsed.
  • Cloud rosters. Roster files live on your Mac. There's no hosted roster to subscribe to.

Privacy

ContactLogo for Mac does not transmit anything to Simple With Us. The app reads your Address Book on-device, writes logos, and that's it. Crash reports are opt-in and scrubbed of identifiers. Full notice at /privacy.

Need help?

If a batch skipped contacts you expected to match, the cause is almost always roster formatting or Address Book source selection. The support page covers the four checks that catch 90% of misses; if those don't help, write to feedback@simplewithus.com with the roster file you used (a sample row is enough).
