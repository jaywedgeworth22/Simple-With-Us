DealDex

Support

Chain selection, wallet handoff, and known-issue reference. If the four checks below don't fix it, send the output to feedback@simplewithus.com and we'll come back the same day.

The four checks that catch 90% of routing issues

  1. Wallet app installed and unlocked. DealDex hands the swap route to your wallet via the universal-link contract. If the wallet is uninstalled or locked, the handoff fails. Unlock and retry.
  2. Correct chain selected in the chain picker. A token on Solana and a token with the same symbol on Base are not the same contract. Confirm the chain before you route.
  3. iOS default-app picker. If you have multiple wallet apps, iOS may ask which one to open. If you picked the wrong wallet and tapped "Always", long-press the route tile and reset the default.
  4. Feed freshness. Onchain data can lag a few seconds. Pull-to-refresh on the feed; listings that landed since the last poll show up immediately.

Crash log or "Application keeps closing"

If DealDex crashes immediately on launch or after a specific screen, pull a Console log from the Mac (Console.app > iPhone > DealDex) and send the last ~200 lines to feedback@simplewithus.com. If you can repro, note the chain, the token, and your iOS version.

Swap failed after handoff

Once the swap is in your wallet, DealDex is out of the loop. Revert, slippage, gas estimation -- those are wallet-side decisions. If the wallet broadcast the transaction and it reverted, the chain receipt is the source of truth. If the wallet refused to broadcast, that's a wallet-side issue (or a route issue -- email us the route token).

Sending logs to support

The app's Settings screen has a "Send Logs" action (under Diagnostics). It bundles the last few minutes into a single zip, scrubbed of your wallet address, and opens a prefilled email to feedback@simplewithus.com. Attach the zip; we respond usually within a business day.

Contact
