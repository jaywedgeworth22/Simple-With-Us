macOS bot orchestration companion

Drive your fleet from the menu bar.

Native Mac companion to BotFleet iOS. Menu-bar quick-launch, dock presence, and shared notifications.

Visit botfleet.app/mac Support Privacy

What's inside

  • Menu bar presence. Fleet summary always one click away; status icons for each bot.
  • Quick launch. Trigger a manual run without opening the iOS app -- pick a bot, hit Run.
  • Shared notifications. Failures and summary windows show on both Mac and iPhone via the same channel.
  • Dock quick actions. Pin bots to the dock for one-tap access; long-press for the last-run tail.

How it pairs

BotFleet for Mac runs alongside the orchestrator on your Mac. It does not orchestrate on its own -- it shares the orchestrator's local network endpoint and surfaces status to the menu bar.

  1. Install BotFleet for Mac alongside the orchestrator. Both register as Bonjour services on the same host.
  2. Open BotFleet for Mac; it auto-discovers the orchestrator and signs in with the same local token.
  3. The iPhone app finds both over Bonjour; the menu bar shows live fleet status without any extra setup.

Companion, not a replacement. BotFleet for Mac needs the orchestrator on the same Mac. It does not host schedules or secrets on its own.

What it doesn't do

  • Cloud relay. No remote dashboard. The Mac is the source of truth.
  • Bot authoring. Companion only. Bring your own scripts to the orchestrator.
  • Background push. Notifications use macOS user-facing delivery -- no daemon-style polling.

Privacy

BotFleet for Mac does not transmit anything to Simple With Us. The app talks to the orchestrator on your Mac, period. Crash reports are opt-in and scrubbed of identifiers. Full notice at /privacy.

Need help?

If the menu bar icon never appears, the LaunchAgent is usually the cause. The support page covers the four checks that catch 90% of companion-launch failures; if those don't help, write to feedback@simplewithus.com with the output of launchctl list | grep botfleet.
