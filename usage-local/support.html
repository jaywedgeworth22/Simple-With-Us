Usage Local Monitor

Support

Local store, manual entry, and known-issue reference. If the four checks below don't fix it, send the output to feedback@simplewithus.com and we'll come back the same day.

The four checks that catch 90% of cap-warning issues

  1. Cap set in the Providers screen. If the cap field is empty, the warning never fires. Open the provider, scroll to Cap, enter the monthly limit, and save.
  2. Daily spend logged today. The rollup uses today's entry. If you forgot to log a session, today's spend is missing and the cap looks further away than it is.
  3. Notification permission granted. Settings > Notifications > Usage Local Monitor > Allow Notifications. Without this, the local warning fires silently.
  4. Device date and timezone. The app rolls by local date. If your travel changed timezone mid-day, today's entry may split across two days.

Crash log or "Application keeps closing"

If Usage Local Monitor crashes immediately on launch or after a specific screen, pull a Console log from the Mac (Console.app > iPhone > Usage Local Monitor) and send the last ~200 lines to feedback@simplewithus.com. If you can repro, note the exact steps and the iOS version (Settings > General > About).

Lost data after a device wipe

Usage Local Monitor does not sync. If you wiped the device without exporting, the local store is gone. The next release adds an iCloud Drive backup toggle; for now, export CSV from the Diagnostics screen before any major device change.

Sending logs to support

The app's Settings screen has a "Send Logs" action (under Diagnostics). It bundles the last few minutes into a single zip, scrubbed of your provider names and caps, and opens a prefilled email to feedback@simplewithus.com. Attach the zip; we respond usually within a business day.

Contact

feedback@simplewithus.com -- open a thread, don't worry about templates. Note your iOS version and which provider the cap warning was supposed to fire on.
