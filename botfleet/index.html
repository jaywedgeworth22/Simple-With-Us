Run your bot fleet from one place.

Bot orchestration platform -- schedule, monitor, and inspect automation fleets from one place. Native iOS app and a macOS menu-bar companion.

What's inside

  • Fleet dashboard. Every bot on one screen -- health, last run, next scheduled, alert state.
  • Schedule. Cron-style schedules per bot, with timezone awareness and per-run overrides.
  • Run inspector. Stream logs from a live run, jump to failures, copy tracebacks in one tap.
  • macOS companion. Menu-bar quick launch and shared notifications for failures and summary windows.
  • Widget surface. Lock-screen and home-screen widgets for the bots you care about most.

How it pairs

BotFleet iOS talks to the BotFleet orchestrator running on your Mac. The orchestrator is the source of truth -- schedules, secrets, run history all live there. The iOS app is a thin client over your local network.

  1. On the Mac: open the BotFleet orchestrator and confirm it's reachable. You'll see its Bonjour URL.
  2. On the iPhone: open BotFleet, tap "Pair", and scan the QR the Mac shows. (Or use Manual entry.)
  3. That's it. The fleet shows up immediately. Settings remembers the pair; Settings > Forget Pairing starts over.

Wire protocol: the iOS app and the Mac orchestrator implement the same contract documented in the open-source repo, so neither side changes shape without the other. botfleet.app.

Mac version

BotFleet for Mac is the menu-bar companion that runs alongside the orchestrator on the same Mac. Quick-launch bots from the menu bar, see fleet status at a glance, and share notifications with the iOS app. Companion, not a replacement -- it does not host schedules or secrets on its own.

Direct download (DMG). Email feedback@simplewithus.com for the build link until the notarized installer is published.

What it doesn't do

  • Cloud relay. Both sides have to be on the same network. No traffic flows through Simple With Us.
  • Bot authoring. We orchestrate; we don't write the bots. Bring your own scripts.
  • Push for every run. Notifications are failures plus daily/weekly summary windows, not per-run pings.

Privacy

BotFleet does not transmit anything to Simple With Us. The app talks to the Mac you paired, period. Crash reports are opt-in and scrubbed of identifiers. Full notice at /privacy.

Need help?

The orchestrator can sometimes silently fail to start. The support page covers the four checks that catch 90% of pair failures; if those don't help, write to feedback@simplewithus.com with the output of launchctl list | grep botfleet from your Mac.
