Privacy notice

What we collect, and what we don't.

Effective 23 September 2026. Operator: Simple With Us, mailing address feedback@simplewithus.com. This notice covers simplewithus.com and every app shipped under a com.simplewithus.* bundle identifier.

The short version

If a feature in any of our apps needs to send data off your device, it asks first. We don't run ad networks, we don't sell usage data, and we don't profile you. Where we do collect something, it's scoped to making the thing you asked for work.

What this site collects

Static pages. We don't run analytics on simplewithus.com -- there is no tracking pixel, no fingerprint script, no third-party tag manager. The only data the site generates is the standard web-server access log (request URL, response code, your IP for the duration of the request, and the User-Agent string), kept for 30 days to detect abuse and then rotated out.

What each app collects

MiniMax Remote (com.simplewithus.minimaxremote)

  • On-device: the companion server address and pair token you save, in iOS-protected storage; the seed used to rotate that token.
  • Off-device: nothing is sent to Simple With Us. The app talks to the Mac you paired; the Mac talks to your own MiniMax Code session. We are not in that path.
  • Crash reports: opt-in. If you turn them on, the standard Apple toolchain surfaces stack traces without your identifiers.

CodeCaps Companion (com.simplewithus.codecaps.ios) and CodeCaps for Mac (com.simplewithus.codecaps.macos)

  • On-device: the per-service balance state and rate-limit windows you've authorised. All local by default.
  • Cross-device sync: opt-in, end-to-end encrypted via your iCloud Keychain. If you don't turn it on, your state never leaves the device you installed it on.
  • Crash reports: same opt-in model.

Third-party services we route through

  • Email. Inbound and outbound email for the studio runs through a third-party email provider. Their privacy policy applies to the message headers and bodies you send to feedback@simplewithus.com and to anything we send back.
  • App Store Connect. Apple's developer portal, used only to publish apps and process TestFlight distribution. Apple's developer privacy policy applies there.

We do not run or rent any analytics, ads, retargeting, attribution, or fingerprinting service. If we ever add one, this notice will say so explicitly and the change will be highlighted in every affected app's release notes.

Your rights

For any data we hold -- which, in practice, is whatever you wrote to us -- you can ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write feedback@simplewithus.com; we respond within seven days. EU/UK residents get the full set of rights under GDPR; California residents get the equivalent under CCPA/CPRA. We are a tiny studio, so "data we hold" rarely amounts to much.

Data retention

Email correspondence is kept for as long as it's useful for the thread, then archived on encrypted disk for up to two years, then deleted. App-side crash logs, if you opt in, are retained by Apple's crash service for 90 days per Apple's policy.

Children's privacy

None of these products are aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided us data, write feedback@simplewithus.com and we'll remove it.

International transfers

simplewithus.com is hosted on Cloudflare Pages (United States + global edge). If you contact us by email, your message may transit providers in your region and ours. We rely on standard contractual clauses for any transfers that require them.

Changes to this notice

When this notice changes materially, we'll bump the effective date at the top and surface a one-line summary inside each affected app's release notes the next time you open it.
