Socratic.Trade

Support

Broker handoff, watchlist, and known-issue reference. If the four checks below don't fix it, send the output to feedback@simplewithus.com and we'll come back the same day.

The four checks that catch 90% of handoff issues

  1. Is your broker app installed? Socratic.Trade hands orders off via the iOS universal-link contract. If you uninstalled the broker app or never installed it, the handoff silently fails. Reinstall, sign in once, then retry.
  2. iOS default-app picker. iOS may prompt "Open in..." the first time you hand off. If you picked the wrong app and tapped "Always", the next handoff uses that bad choice. Long-press the order tile, choose your broker, and confirm "Always".
  3. News feed freshness. If your news hasn't refreshed in days, pull-to-refresh on the watchlist. The app pulls on launch and on pull; nothing happens in the background.
  4. Position CSV import. If imports fail, the most common cause is a non-UTF-8 CSV with a stray comma inside a quoted broker name. Re-export from your broker and try again; the support inbox can validate a sample.

Crash log or "Application keeps closing"

If Socratic.Trade crashes immediately on launch or after a specific screen, pull a Console log from the Mac (Console.app > iPhone > Socratic.Trade) and send the last ~200 lines to feedback@simplewithus.com. If you can repro, note the exact steps and the iOS version (Settings > General > About).

Broker order didn't fill

Once the handoff opens your broker app, Socratic.Trade is no longer in the loop. The fill, the partial fill, the rejection -- all of that lives in the broker's audit trail. Check your broker app's order history; if the symbol was wrong, that's our bug (email us). If the order was rejected by the broker, that's a broker-side issue.

Sending logs to support

The app's Settings screen has a "Send Logs" action (under Diagnostics). It bundles the last few minutes into a single zip, scrubbed of your watchlist names, and opens a prefilled email to feedback@simplewithus.com. Attach the zip; we respond usually within a business day.
