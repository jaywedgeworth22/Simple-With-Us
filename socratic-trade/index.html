iOS trading companion

Track your US equity positions, follow the news that matters, and execute through your broker -- not ours.

Socratic.Trade is the iOS companion for US equity traders. Watchlist, news, disclosure feed, and earnings calendar, all in one app. When you want to act, the app hands off to your existing broker so your custody never leaves the relationship you already trust.

What's inside

  • Position tracker. Manual entry, or import from any broker that exports a CSV. Cost basis is held locally and rolls up across lots.
  • News with relevance ranking. Per-ticker news streams surface catalysts before they hit the broad wires. Read the source; ignore the noise.
  • Watchlist with sector overlays. Pin your tickers; see sector moves alongside single-name stories so you can tell company news from industry drift.
  • Earnings and disclosure calendar. Upcoming earnings, dividend ex-dates, and material 8-K filings, scheduled to your local timezone.
  • Execution handoff. Tap an order and the app opens your broker with the symbol prefilled. You confirm there. The app never touches your funds.

How it works

Socratic.Trade does not connect to your brokerage directly. The app holds your watchlist, position notes, and cost basis on-device. When you want to act, it hands the order back to whichever broker app you've installed -- the one that already has your account, your funds, and your approvals. That keeps custody where it belongs and lets you keep the broker you already use.

  1. Add your tickers to a watchlist. The app pulls the news feed and earnings calendar on first launch.
  2. Optionally import a position CSV from your broker to track cost basis and P/L.
  3. When you're ready to trade, tap the order tile. The app hands off to your broker with the symbol and quantity prefilled.

What it doesn't do

  • Direct execution. We never hold your funds or place orders on your behalf. The app hands off to your broker.
  • Options or futures. Equity-only on the v1 path. Multi-leg options and futures come later.
  • Investment advice. The news and calendar are surfaces; the calls are yours.

Privacy

Socratic.Trade keeps your watchlist, positions, and notes on-device. Broker handoffs use the universal iOS open-url contract; the app never sees your account login. Crash reports are opt-in and scrubbed of identifiers. Full notice at /privacy.

Need help?

The most common hiccup is the broker handoff not opening the right app -- that's almost always a default-app choice in iOS Settings. The support page covers the four checks that catch 90% of pair-up issues; if those don't help, write to feedback@simplewithus.com with your iOS version and broker app name.
