What's inside

  • Per-model caps. Daily and weekly caps across every provider you subscribe to, on one screen.
  • Daily spend. Real spend, broken out by model and by day. No estimates, no projections.
  • Rate-limit warnings. A heads-up before you hit the limit -- not after the request fails.
  • Provider roster. Switch providers without losing the per-model breakdown.

How it works

CodeCaps Companion reads usage from the providers you connect, rolls up the numbers per model, and shows you what's left in the current window. Numbers refresh on launch and on pull-to-refresh; there is no background polling.

  1. Add a provider from the in-app list and paste your API key. Keys live in the iOS Keychain.
  2. Open the dashboard. Caps and daily spend show immediately.
  3. Tap a model to drill into per-day history. Pin the ones you care about to the top.

Privacy: your provider keys live in the iOS Keychain. Usage numbers are stored on-device. The app does not call home. Full notice at /privacy.

What it doesn't do

  • Cross-device sync. Caps and history live on this iPhone. There is no account and no cloud mirror.
  • Forecasting. We show what you used, not what you'll use. Predicting future spend is a different job.
  • Provider auth mediation. We never proxy your calls. The app reads usage only; you make requests elsewhere.

Privacy

CodeCaps Companion does not transmit anything to Simple With Us. Provider keys live in the iOS Keychain; usage history lives on this iPhone. Crash reports are opt-in and scrubbed of identifiers. Full notice at /privacy.

Need help?

If a provider's numbers look stale or wrong, the cause is almost always a revoked or expired key. The support page covers the four checks that catch 90% of stale-data reports; if those don't help, write to feedback@simplewithus.com with the provider name and the timestamp of the last good fetch.
