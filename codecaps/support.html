CodeCaps Companion

Support

Provider keys, refresh behavior, and known-issue reference. If the four checks below don't fix it, send the output to feedback@simplewithus.com and we'll come back the same day.

The four checks that catch 90% of stale-data reports

  1. iOS: provider configured. Open Settings > Providers. Each provider you want caps on must be added here, with its key pasted. Keys are masked after entry; tap Reveal to confirm.
  2. Provider: key still works. Open the provider's dashboard in Safari and confirm the same key returns usage. If the dashboard says the key is revoked, regenerate and re-paste in the app.
  3. iOS: Background refresh. The app pulls usage on launch and pull-to-refresh only; if you don't see the app for a week, numbers will be a week old. Settings > General > Background App Refresh -- enable for CodeCaps Companion if you want fresher data on open.
  4. iOS: app version. Open Settings > Version. If you're on an old TestFlight build, the per-model mapping can lag behind the provider's current model names. Update via TestFlight.

Crash log or "Application keeps closing"

If CodeCaps Companion crashes immediately on launch or while pulling usage, pull a Console log from the Mac (Console.app > iPhone > CodeCaps Companion) and send the last ~200 lines to feedback@simplewithus.com. If you can also repro the crash, note the steps and the provider you were viewing.

Caps look wrong

This is almost always a provider-side change -- new model names, new limits, or a reset window. Confirm by opening the provider's official dashboard in Safari and comparing the same window. If our numbers diverge from the dashboard by more than 5%, send a screenshot of both to feedback@simplewithus.com.

Sending logs to support

The app's Settings screen has a "Send Logs" action (under Diagnostics). It bundles the last few minutes into a single zip, scrubbed of your provider keys, and opens a prefilled email to feedback@simplewithus.com. Attach the zip; we respond usually within a business day.

Contact

feedback@simplewithus.com -- open a thread, don't worry about templates. Link to your TestFlight build if you have it (Settings > Version > Build).
